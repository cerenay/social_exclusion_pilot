T1 - no identity, random exclusion

Now we start Part 2 of the study.

You will be asked to perform a task that involves moving a slider to a position corresponding to a displayed range of numbers indicated.

You will see a screen with 5 sliders: each slider is initially positioned at 0 and can be moved as far as 100. When you move the slider, you will see a number showing its current position. Your task is to position each slider at a number within the indicated range.

There are two versions of this task: an easy task and a hard task. The two versions differ only in the size of the number range you need to match on the slider.

Another participant will also complete the task. One of you will be assigned the easy task and the other one will be assigned the hard task. The tasks are randomly assigned by a computer.

You will receive additional EMUs for completing the task. Both participants must complete the tasks to continue to Part 3.
