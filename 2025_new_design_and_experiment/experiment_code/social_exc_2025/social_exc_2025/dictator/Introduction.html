Now we start Part 3. You will be randomly and anonymously paired with a new participant.

The participant who assigned the tasks in Part 2 will not participate in this part of the study.

You will be asked to make decisions in 3 rounds. In each round, you will have a certain number of EMU. You will be asked to allocate these EMUs between yourself and the other participant.

Each round presents a different decision situation, which we call scenarios. The three rounds differ in the information provided about the other participant.
