Now we start Part 2 of the study.

You will be assigned to a task that you will complete with another participant. The other participant may or may not belong to your group (dynamic Kandisky or Klee).

All participants will be asked to perform a task that involves moving a slider to a position corresponding to a displayed range of numbers.

There are two versions of this task: an easy task and a hard task. The two versions differ only in the size of the number range you need to match on the slider.

Within each pair, one participant will complete the easy version and the other will complete the hard version. You may be assigned to either version.

Assignment to the task version is made by an independent participant, who may or may not belong to your painting group. This participant only knows to which painting group each participant belongs to.

For completing the task correctly, you will earn X EMUs.

All participants must complete their tasks in order to continue to Part 3 of the study, where additional rewards can be earned.
