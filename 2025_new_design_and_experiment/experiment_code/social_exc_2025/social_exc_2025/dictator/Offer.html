There is 20 EMUs to be allocated between two roles:

  • Player A (Allocator): decides how much of the 20 EMUs to send to Player B. Player A keeps the remainder.
  • Player B (Recipient): makes no decision and receives whatever Player A sends.

You will be playing as Allocator . Therefore, You decide how many EMUs to send to the other participant and how many EMUs you keep to yourself.

After you submit your decision, your role (A or B) will be randomly assigned with equal probability (50/50).

After everyone finishes recording their decisions, the computer will randomly select one of the three rounds for payoff calculations. Next, the computer will randomly select one of the decision scenarios from that round. Each round of decisions and scenarios will have an equal chance of being chosen.
