In this part of the study, you will be randomly and anonymously paired with a new participant.

The participants who assigned task versions in the previous part do not take part in this part of the study.

You will make decisions in several rounds. In each round, you will decide how to allocate a certain number of EMUs between yourself and the other participant.

Each round presents a different decision situation.

At the end of the study, one round will be randomly selected and the decision you made in that round will determine your earnings for this part.
