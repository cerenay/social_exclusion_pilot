In this part of the study, you will be placed in a team of two participants.

The other team member may or may not belong to the same painting group as you.

All team members will complete a task that involves moving a slider to a position corresponding to a displayed range of numbers.

There are two versions of this task: an easy task and a hard task.

The two versions differ only in the size of the number range you need to match on the slider.

Within each team, one participant will complete the easy version and the other will complete the hard version. You may be assigned to either version.

Assignment to the task version is made by an independent participant, who may or may not belong to your painting group. This participant only knows the painting group of each team member.

For completing the task correctly, you will earn X EMUs.

Both team members must complete their tasks in order to continue to Part 3 of the study, where additional rewards can be earned.
