In this part, you will be assigned to a team of 2 members. The team members may or may not belong to the same painting group as you (GROUP).

All team members will be asked to perform a task in which they need to click on a slider in the position corresponding to a given range of numbers.

There are two types of tasks: an easy task and a hard task. The tasks differ in the size of the number range to click on the slider.

One of the players in your team will perform the easy task, while the other will perform the hard task. You can be assigned to either task.

The assignment to the type of task is made by an independent player who may or may not be from your painting group.

The independent player only knows which painting group each team member belongs to.

For performing the task correctly, you will earn X EMUs. Note that all members of the team must complete their tasks to move on to Part 3 of the study and earn additional rewards.
