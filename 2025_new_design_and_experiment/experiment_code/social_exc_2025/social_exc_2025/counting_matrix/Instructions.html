Now we start Part 2 of the study.

You will be assigned to a team of 2 members. The team members may or may not belong to the same painting group as you (GROUP).

All team members will be asked to perform a task in which they need to click on a slider in a position corresponding to a given range of numbers.

There are two types of tasks: an easy task and a hard task. The tasks differ in the size of the number range.

One of the players in your team will perform the easy task, while the other(s) will perform the hard task. You can be assigned to either task.

The assignment to the task is made by an independent player who may or may not be from your painting group and only knows which painting group each participant is assigned in Part 1 of the study.

For performing the task correctly, you will earn X EMUs. Note that all members of the team must complete their tasks to move on to the next stage and earn additional rewards.
