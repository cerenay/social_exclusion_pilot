Now we start Part 2 of the study.

You will be assigned to a team of 2 members. The group members may or may not belong to the same painting group as you (GROUP).

All group members will be asked to perform a task in which they count how many number 0s are in a matrix.

There are two types of tasks: an easy task and a hard task. The tasks differ in the size of the matrix.

One of the players in your group will perform the easy task, while the other(s) will perform the hard task. You can be assigned to either task.

The assignment to the task is made by an independent spectator who may or may not be from your group and only knows which painting each participant chose in the first stage.

For performing the task correctly, you will earn X EMUs. Note that all members of the group must complete their tasks to move on to the next stage and earn additional rewards.
